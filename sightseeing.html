Welcome To Pawna

Welcome to our hotel situated in the very heart of Lonavala, just moments away from its most iconic tourist spots.

Immerse yourself in the rich history of the region with visits to Lohagad Fort and the ancient marvels of Bhaja Caves. Experience comfort and convenience like never before during your stay with us.

Pawna Lake

Imagine yourself, relaxing surrounded by nature and ancient forts near our cottages in Lonavala. It's like a magnet for camping fans, with spectacular views of the mountains and sunsets. That's Pawna Lake for you!

Pawna Lake is like a nature's paradise, full of water and surrounded by lush greenery and wildlife. It's not just a sightseeing spot, but also a perfect place to camp, especially if you're staying at Green Velvet Resort. It's the ultimate weekend escape!

Lonavala Sightseeing

Tung Fort

Tung fort is a mesmerizing destination in pune near Green Velvet Resort. The fort is specifically admired for its breathtaking views and spectacular treks. Since Pawna was built, it is now surrounded by water on three sides. One can ferry across in a boat from Pawna dam to its base village, Tungi.

Lonavala Sightseeing

Lohagad Fort

Lohagad fort is one of the best places to visit in Lonavala. It is located at the top of a hill and provides excellent views of temples and lakes. Another appealing feature of this location is its proximity to Pune and Mumbai, which attracts visitors all year.

Lonavala Sightseeing

Bhaja Caves Sightseeing

The Bhaja caves are a collection of 22 rock-cut Buddhist caves near Pune. The Hinayana sect of Buddhism built the Bhaja Caves near Lonavala in Maharashtra. The sculptures in the Bhaja caves feature grand headdresses, jewellery, and garlands that were originally painted in bright colours but were later covered by plaster.

More Places to Discover

Tucked into the Sahyadri hills of Maval, Green Velvet Resort is an easy base for exploring the best of Pawna Lake and Lonavala. Spend the morning climbing a hill fort, the afternoon among ancient rock-cut caves, and come back to the resort in time for sunset.

Forts & TreksMonsoon & winter

Tikona Fort

A pyramid-shaped hill fort rising right beside Pawna Lake. A steady climb rewards you with sweeping views of the lake, Tung Fort and the Maval countryside.

Hill fort · Easy–moderate trekDirections
Forts & TreksMonsoon

Visapur Fort

Lohagad's larger twin on the same plateau. In the monsoon its rocky trail turns into a string of small waterfalls, making it a favourite with trekkers.

Hill fort · Moderate trekDirections
Forts & TreksWinter & late May

Rajmachi Fort

Twin fortresses, Shrivardhan and Manaranjan, look out over the Konkan valley. Known for overnight treks and the firefly season just before the monsoon.

Twin forts · Day or overnight trekDirections
Forts & TreksOctober – February

Duke's Nose

A sharp cliff in Khandala named for its likeness to the Duke of Wellington's nose. Popular for treks, rock climbing and rappelling, with wide views over the ghats.

Cliff · Trek & rock climbingDirections
Caves & HeritageAll year

Karla Caves & Ekvira Temple

Ancient Buddhist rock-cut caves with one of the largest and best-preserved chaitya (prayer) halls in India. The Ekvira Devi temple stands beside them on the same hill.

Heritage · Short climbDirections
Caves & HeritageAll year

Bedse Caves

A quieter group of Buddhist rock-cut caves known for their finely carved pillars and stupa hall, reached by a short flight of steps through the Maval hills.

Heritage · Short climbDirections
Views & WaterfallsMonsoon

Bhushi Dam

Lonavala's best-loved monsoon spot, where overflowing water cascades down the dam's steps and visitors sit in the gentle flow.

Dam · Monsoon favouriteDirections
Views & WaterfallsMonsoon & winter

Tiger's Point

Also called Tiger's Leap, this cliff-edge viewpoint drops steeply into the valley. Come for sunsets, drifting monsoon clouds and seasonal waterfalls.

ViewpointDirections
Views & WaterfallsAll year

Lion's Point

A wide viewpoint on the road towards Aamby Valley, loved for its open views of the Sahyadri ranges at sunrise and sunset.

ViewpointDirections
Views & WaterfallsMonsoon

Kune Falls

A tall, multi-tiered waterfall near Khandala that roars to life in the monsoon months, framed by deep green valleys.

WaterfallDirections
Adventure & LeisureOctober – May

Kamshet Paragliding

Kamshet is one of India's best-known paragliding destinations. Tandem flights with certified pilots let first-timers soar above the Maval hills.

Della Adventure Park

A large adventure park in Lonavala with ziplines, ATV rides, giant swings and plenty more for families and groups.

Celebrity Wax Museum

Life-size wax figures of film stars, sports icons and world leaders make for a fun family stop in Lonavala.
